About us

A small, technical studio in Croydon

We help local businesses and founders use technology properly: websites that bring in customers, automations that save hours, and AI that's genuinely useful, not just hype.

Pictoflex Labs was founded by Ronak Parmar, a developer with a background in DevOps and cloud infrastructure who studies Mathematics & Statistics alongside running the studio.

We've built and launched our own product, Yumo, a finance app for side hustlers and the self-employed, so we know what it takes to get something from idea to real users. We've also built a mobile workforce app for a security company and done SEO and Google Business Profile work for a London therapy clinic.

Our approach is simple: look first, then build only what pays for itself. Every project starts with a free mini-audit and a fixed price in writing. You own everything we build, and monthly plans come with no long contracts.

We're based in Croydon and work with businesses across South London, London and the rest of the UK.

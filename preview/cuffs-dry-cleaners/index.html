FAQ

Good to know

Where are you?

578 Streatham High Road, Streatham, London SW16 3QQ, on the High Road between Streatham Common and Norbury.

What are your opening hours?

Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 7:30am to 6:30pm, Wednesday 7:30am to 2:30pm, Saturday 8am to 6pm. Closed on Sunday.

Can you remove stains?

Yes. Point out any stains when you drop off and we'll treat them before cleaning. Some stains can't be fully removed, and we'll always tell you honestly.

How much does dry cleaning cost?

It depends on the item and fabric. Call 020 8764 0317 or pop in and we'll give you a price before we start.

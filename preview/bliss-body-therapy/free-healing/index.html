Free Healing

Giving back. Growing together.

One of the most beautiful purposes of being part of a society is to support one another in growth.

I believe that one of the most beautiful purposes of being part of a society is to support one another in growth — to help each other move from fear towards trust, from limitation towards possibility, and from simply surviving towards fully experiencing our potential.

We are not here only for ourselves. Sometimes a simple act of presence, encouragement, touch or shared practice can become a turning point in another person's journey. This is why I have chosen to offer some experiences freely and openly, as a way of giving back and creating a space where people can meet, connect and grow together.

The Story of LadyBird

LadyBird

Some years ago, I experienced a traditional Vision Quest — a period of solitude and deep inner exploration. The experience became an important turning point in my own journey.

As a result, I received my shamanic name: LadyBird.

In many traditions, the ladybird is associated with healing, good fortune, protection and the arrival of something positive. I have embraced this symbol as a reminder of the energy I wish to bring into my work — lightness, transformation, trust and the possibility of a new beginning.

Morning Active Meditation

6:00

I invite you to join me for free morning active meditation practices at 6:00 am. It is a chance to begin the day differently — before the noise, responsibilities and demands of everyday life take over.

Through movement, breathing, awareness and active meditation, we awaken the body, release what no longer needs to be carried and create space for a new quality of energy.

You don't need to be experienced in meditation. You simply need to come as you are.

We move together.

We rise together.

Because perhaps our greatest potential is not something we achieve alone. It is something we help awaken in one another.

Your right to stop at any moment

You are in control throughout the session. You can pause, redirect or end the session at any moment — no explanation required. Your comfort and sense of safety come before anything else.

At Bliss Body Therapy, energetic healing is based on the idea that the body is more than muscles, bones and organs. We also experience ourselves through an electromagnetic field of information — a subtle dimension through which the body, emotions, sensations and our inner state are interconnected.

This approach invites you to experience the body in a different way: not simply as something to be treated, but as something to be felt, listened to and awakened. Through conscious touch, subtle movement, sensory stimulation, breath and focused attention, energetic treatments create an opportunity to slow down and reconnect with yourself.

For me, energetic healing is about bringing energy back into places that have become numb, contracted or disconnected — awakening the body's natural sensitivity and reminding us that feeling alive is an embodied experience.

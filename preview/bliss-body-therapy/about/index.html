About

A journey from the mind to the body.

My journey began in Estonia — and led me somewhere I could not have predicted.

I'm Natalia, the founder of Bliss Body Therapy. My journey began in Estonia, where I spent much of my life and worked for five years in a legal firm. Moving to England opened a completely new chapter — one that led me into the world of massage, bodywork and human potential.

Over the years, my curiosity about the human being has taken me far beyond traditional massage. I have explored energy practices, meditation, conscious movement and touch, while developing a deep interest in neuroscience, neurochemistry and the fascinating connection between the body, mind and our inner state.

Through years of practice and exploration, I began to bring these different worlds together, creating an approach that is uniquely my own.

The Approach

Come back to your body.
Come back to life.

We are living in a time of unprecedented pressure. Our minds are constantly stimulated, our attention is pulled in countless directions, and the virtual world offers endless ways to escape. In response, many of us unconsciously contract — our muscles tighten, our breathing becomes shallow, our senses become quieter.

But there is another way. The body is not something we need to escape from. It is the doorway. The body holds our sensations, emotions, vitality and capacity for pleasure. When we begin to soften, breathe and truly inhabit it again, something changes. We become more present. More connected. More alive.

Bliss is not an escape from life. Bliss is coming fully back into it.

FAQ

Questions drivers ask us

How quickly can you get to me?

Our typical call-out time is around 20 to 25 minutes in and around Harrow and Wembley. Further away it depends on distance and traffic. We'll give you an honest arrival time when you call.

How much will it cost?

It depends on your tyre size and how far we need to travel, so we give every customer a quote first. Call, or send us your tyre size and location on WhatsApp, and we'll reply with a price.

Where do I find my tyre size?

It's printed on the side of the tyre and looks like 205/55 R16 91V. Send us a photo of it on WhatsApp and we'll do the rest.

Can you repair my puncture or do I need a new tyre?

Many punctures in the main tread can be repaired. Damage to the sidewall or shoulder, or a tyre that's been driven on flat, usually can't. We'll check it and tell you honestly.

I've lost my locking wheel nut key. Can you help?

Yes. We remove locking wheel nuts and rounded or seized bolts, then fit standard bolts so you're not stuck next time.

Do you really work 24/7?

Yes, including nights and weekends. We also offer an emergency service for when you need us urgently.

How can I pay?

Card, bank transfer or cash, whichever is easiest for you.

How far do you travel?

We're based in Harrow and Wembley and cover every London postcode, plus HA and UB, and up to 50 miles out, including Hertfordshire, Buckinghamshire, Essex, Kent and Surrey.

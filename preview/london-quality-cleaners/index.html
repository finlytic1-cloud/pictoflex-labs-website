FAQ

Questions we're often asked

Do you offer same-day dry cleaning?

Yes, for many items. Bring them in early or call us on 020 8689 0529 to check availability for urgent cleaning or alterations.

Can you collect and deliver my clothes?

Yes. Request a pickup online or by phone and we'll collect, clean and deliver your items back to your door.

Do you clean wedding dresses?

Yes. We clean and preserve wedding dresses with the care delicate fabrics, beading and lace need.

What alterations do you do?

Shortening, lengthening, taking in, letting out, tapering, zips, hems and custom adjustments for a precise fit.

Can you clean curtains and duvets?

Yes. We clean curtains, duvets and other home furnishings using methods suited to each fabric.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8:30am to 6:30pm. We're closed on Sundays.

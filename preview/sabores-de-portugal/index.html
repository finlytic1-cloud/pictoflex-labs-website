FAQ

Good to know

Can I eat in?

Yes. We have a seating area, so you can enjoy your coffee and pastries in, or take them away.

Do you sell Portuguese groceries?

Yes. Our mini-market stocks Portuguese groceries and deli products, so you can take a taste of home with you.

Do you sell Portuguese wine and beer?

Yes, to enjoy with food or take home. We operate Challenge 25, so please bring ID if you look under 25.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 7am to 6pm, Saturday 8am to 6pm, and Sunday 8am to 2pm.

FAQ

Questions you're probably asking

If your question isn't here, book a call and we'll answer it live.

Do you work with local businesses, not just startups? +

Yes. We work with clinics, salons, trades, estate agents and other local businesses across London. Most start with a small fix to their website or Google profile, then add automations or an AI receptionist once they see the results.

Will an AI receptionist replace my staff? +

No. It handles the calls your team can't get to: when they're busy, at lunch or after hours. Your staff still look after the people in front of them, and anything tricky is passed straight to you.

How do you keep prices so low? +

We're a lean team, not a bloated agency with 40 people and fancy offices. No account managers, no project coordinators — you talk directly to the people building your product. That means lower overheads and better prices for you.

What tech do you use? +

Flutter for mobile apps (one codebase, both platforms), Node.js/Express for backends, React or plain HTML/CSS for websites, and MongoDB or PostgreSQL for databases. We pick the best tool for your project, not the trendiest one.

Do I own the code? +

Yes. 100%. Once the project is delivered and paid for, you own everything — source code, designs, assets, IP. It's yours to keep, modify, or hand to another developer if you ever want to.

What if I need changes after launch? +

That's what the retainer is for. We offer monthly maintenance plans for ongoing updates, bug fixes, and new features. Or you can book us for one-off changes — whatever works for you.

How long does a typical project take? +

Website quick fixes: 3 days. New websites: 1-2 weeks. Automations and AI receptionists: live within a week. MVPs: 4-6 weeks. Full mobile apps: 8-12 weeks. We'll give you an exact timeline before we start, and we stick to it.

Can I see progress during the build? +

Absolutely. We work in weekly sprints and share updates every week. You'll see what's been built, what's next, and you can give feedback as we go. No surprises at the end.

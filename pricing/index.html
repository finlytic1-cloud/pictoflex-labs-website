FAQ

Pricing questions

Are there any contracts? +

No. Monthly plans run month to month and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

Do I own my website and accounts? +

Yes. Your domain, website, Google profile and data are yours. We just look after them.

Are text messages and AI call minutes included? +

Yes, with sensible fair-use limits that cover most small businesses. If you regularly go over, we'll talk to you before anything changes.

Can I start small and upgrade later? +

Of course. Most clients start on Care or a single add-on and upgrade once they see results. Setup fees you've already paid count towards a bigger plan.

Do you charge VAT? +

Prices shown are what you pay. We'll confirm everything in writing before we start.
